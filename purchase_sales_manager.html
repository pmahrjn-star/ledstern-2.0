Google Sheets Setup

Connect your Google Sheet to start managing purchases and sales. You'll need a Google API key and your Spreadsheet ID.

1
Get a Google API Key
Go to console.cloud.google.com → APIs & Services → Credentials → Create API Key. Enable the Google Sheets API for your project.
2
Find your Spreadsheet ID
Open your Google Sheet. The ID is in the URL between /d/ and /edit:
docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/YOUR_ID_HERE/edit
3
Sheet names
Enter the exact tab names for your purchases and sales sheets (e.g. "Purchases" and "Sales")
